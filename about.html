About Me

I'm a computer science graduate who has always enjoyed technology and figuring out how things work. I like solving problems, learning new things, and finding practical ways to use technology rather than just learning about it in a classroom.

My background is a mix of computer science and real-world security experience. Through my degree, I've worked with software development, databases, cybersecurity, networking, and data using technologies like Java, Python, SQL, JavaScript, SQLite, and MongoDB. I've also built applications and worked on projects involving secure development and information security.

Outside of school, working in physical security has given me a different perspective on security as a whole. I've gained hands-on experience with access control, monitoring, identifying potential risks, following security procedures, documenting incidents, and responding when something doesn't seem right. That experience has helped me understand that security isn't only about protecting systems and data—it also involves people, processes, and the physical environment around those systems.

Although I'm still new to the professional technology field, I'm eager to learn and grow. I enjoy learning new things, challenging myself, and building skills in areas I haven't worked with before. I don't expect to know everything starting out, but I'm willing to put in the time to learn, ask questions, and keep improving. I'm excited to continue gaining hands-on experience and see where my career in technology takes me.

I'm using this portfolio to share what I've learned so far, the projects I've built, and the new things I continue to work on as I grow professionally.
